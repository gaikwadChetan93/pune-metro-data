About Pune Metro Guide

An independent guide to the Pune Metro. Not affiliated with Maha Metro, Puneri Metro or PMRDA.

Independent and unofficial

Pune Metro Guide is an independent, unofficial guide to the Pune Metro, made in Pune. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Maha Metro, Puneri Metro, PMRDA or any government body, and it does not sell tickets.

Where the facts come from

Every fare, timing and station fact comes from a source you can check: the operators' official fare charts, timetables, station lists and press releases, news reports (marked as unverified where they are the only source), and OpenStreetMap for station and place positions. Each page lists its sources.

What we don't do

There is no live train tracking, crowding or delay information here, because the operators don't publish it. Trip times are estimates and are labelled as such. Stations and lines are only shown as open once they carry passengers.

Privacy

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. Fonts and scripts are served from this site.

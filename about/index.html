About Pune Metro Guide

Pune Metro Guide is an independent, unofficial guide to the Pune Metro, made in Pune. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Maha Metro, Puneri Metro, PMRDA or any government body, and it does not sell tickets.

Every fare, timing and station fact comes from a source you can check: the operators' official fare charts, timetables, station lists and press releases, news reports (marked as unverified where they are the only source), and OpenStreetMap for station and place positions. Each page lists its sources.

The same data powers the Android app and this website. Spot a mistake? Email care.mobileutility@gmail.com, or use Report a mistake in the app.

Plan this trip in the app

Pune Metro Guide works offline, shows the last train from your station, reminds you before it leaves, and is in English, मराठी and हिंदी. No account, no ads, no tracking.

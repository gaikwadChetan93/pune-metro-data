Hinjawadi to the city by metro

The Pink Line runs from Maan, past the Hinjawadi IT parks, to District Court in about 45 minutes. For the rest of Pune you change there to the Purple Line or Aqua Line, on a second ticket.

Changing at District Court

  1. Ride the Pink Line to District Court, the last stop.
  2. Scan your Pink Line ticket at Puneri Metro's gates to leave.
  3. Follow the signs to the Purple Line and Aqua Line and scan your second ticket at Maha Metro's gates.

Choose the platform by the end of the line you're heading towards:

  • Purple Line towards Swargate: Kasba Peth, Mahatma Phule Mandai and Swargate
  • Purple Line towards PCMC: Shivaji Nagar, Khadki and Bopodi…
  • Aqua Line towards Ramwadi: R.T.O Pune, Pune Railway Station and Ruby Hall Clinic…
  • Aqua Line towards Vanaz: PMC, Chhatrapati Sambhaji Udyan and Deccan Gymkhana…

The Pink Line's Shivaji Nagar platform isn't open yet. For Shivaji Nagar, ride to District Court and take the Purple Line one stop back, towards PCMC, for ₹10 more.

Punekar News reported on 4 November 2026 that joining up the two ticket systems is expected to take another three to four months.

Heading into the city late? The last Pink Line train leaves Maan at 22:30 and takes about 45 minutes to District Court, after Maha Metro's published closing time of 23:00. If you need the Purple Line or Aqua Line, leave Hinjawadi earlier: Maha Metro doesn't publish the last train from each station.

Good to know

  • 4 Pink Line stations aren't open yet: Baner Gaon, Baner Pashan Link Road, Bhosalenagar - RBI and Shivaji Nagar. Pink Line stations
  • Puneri Metro allows bicycles on the Pink Line, and its trains have sections for women only.
  • Each Hinjawadi station has lifts and escalators, according to Puneri Metro's station list.

Questions

Is there a metro from Hinjawadi to Shivajinagar?

Yes, with one change. The Pink Line's Shivaji Nagar platform isn't open yet, so ride to District Court and take the Purple Line one stop back. From Hinjawadi Phase-I it costs ₹49 + ₹10 = ₹59.

How long does Hinjawadi to Pune Station take by metro?

About 34 minutes on the trains from Hinjawadi Phase-I, plus the change at District Court, for ₹49 + ₹10 = ₹59. The time is our estimate from station distances.

Which metro station is nearest Infosys, Wipro or TCS?

Puneri Metro names these on its station exits: TCS: Maan, gate 1; Tech Mahindra: Maan, gate 2; Embassy Quadron: Maan - MIDC Circle Phase-II, gate 1; Infosys: Hinjawadi Phase-II Circle-II, gate 1 and Hinjawadi Phase-II Circle-I, gate 1; Wipro: Hinjawadi Phase-II Circle-I, gate 1; Birla Soft: Shree Shiv Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Chowk Hinjawadi, gate 2.

What time is the last metro from Hinjawadi?

Puneri Metro publishes the last departures from the ends of the line: 22:30 from Maan and 23:40 from District Court.

Do I need two tickets from Hinjawadi?

Only if you leave the Pink Line at District Court for the Purple or Aqua Line, which includes Shivaji Nagar for now. Trips along the Pink Line need one ticket.

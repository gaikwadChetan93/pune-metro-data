Pay less with a student pass or One Pune card

Pay with a One Pune card and Maha Metro takes money off every Purple and Aqua line ride: 30% with the student pass, and 10% or more with the cards anyone can get.

Getting the student pass

Who can get it: students up to graduation level. If you’re under 18, the card is issued in a parent’s or guardian’s name, with their KYC (as reported by the Free Press Journal in July 2025).

  1. Fill in the One Pune Student Pass e-form.
  2. At a Maha Metro station ticket counter, show that you’re a student. When the pass launched in 2023, that meant a student ID card or a bonafide certificate for the current year.
  3. Activate the card at the counter: an OTP comes to your mobile, you enter it on the counter’s card machine, and you set a PIN.
  4. Pay the card fee and top up, and tap the card at the gates from then on.

Using a card

  • Tap the card at the entry gate and again at the exit. The fare is worked out from the two taps and taken from the card’s chip balance, which holds up to ₹2,000.
  • Top up at a station counter, or on the One Pune card customer portal. After an online top-up, put the card in a station’s Add Value Machine so the chip balance updates.
  • Lost your card? Call 022-45000800 straight away to block it. The chip balance can’t be refunded.
  • A card lasts three years. If you don’t use it for a year, the bank switches it off until you top it up.

On the Pink Line

Puneri Metro accepts One Pune cards on the Pink Line, but we haven’t found a published Pink Line discount. On a trip that needs two tickets, tap out of one operator’s gates at District Court and tap in at the other’s.

Questions

Is there a student pass for Pune Metro?

Yes: the One Pune Vidyarthi Pass. It takes 30% off every Maha Metro ride, every day.

How much does the student pass cost?

₹100 for the card and ₹75 a year, plus GST. Topping up costs 1.8% of the amount, up to ₹12.

Is there a weekend discount?

The student pass gives 30% every day. The non-KYC One Pune card gives 30% on Saturdays and Sundays and 10% on weekdays.

Does the One Pune card work on the Pink Line?

Puneri Metro accepts One Pune cards on the Pink Line, but we haven’t found a published Pink Line discount.

What if I lose my One Pune card?

Call 022-45000800 to block it. The chip balance can’t be refunded.

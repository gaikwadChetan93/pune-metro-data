How Pune Metro fares work

Two operators, two fare charts. Here’s what each one charges, when a trip needs two tickets, and the trips where Maha Metro’s chart gives two different fares.

Purple LineAqua LinePink Line₹10–35 · ₹12–62

Two operators, two charts

Each operator prices trips on its own lines with its own station-to-station chart. The fare depends only on where you get on and off.

Trips that need two tickets

A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both. You change at District Court, where you leave one operator’s gates and enter the other’s.

Fares that differ by direction

Paying less

Maha Metro takes money off every Purple and Aqua line ride paid with a One Pune card: 30% with the student pass, 10% with the card anyone can get, and 30% at weekends with the non-KYC card.

Questions

What is the minimum Pune Metro fare?

₹10 on the Purple and Aqua lines and ₹12 on the Pink Line.

What is the maximum Pune Metro fare?

₹35 on the Purple and Aqua lines and ₹62 on the Pink Line. A trip that needs two tickets costs up to ₹92.

Is the fare the same in both directions?

Yes, except for 11 pairs of stations on Maha Metro’s chart, which shows a different fare each way.

How do I check my fare?

Pick your two stations in the journey planner. It shows both tickets for a two-ticket trip.

Why do some trips need two tickets?

The Pink Line is run by Puneri Metro and the Purple and Aqua lines by Maha Metro, and each sells its own tickets.

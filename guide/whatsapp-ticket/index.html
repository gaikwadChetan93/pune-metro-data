Buy a Pune Metro ticket on WhatsApp

One WhatsApp number, +91 94201 01990, sells QR tickets for all three lines. Here's how it works, what to do when a trip needs two tickets, and who to contact if a ticket doesn't arrive.

Open WhatsApp chat+91 94201 01990

The number

+91 94201 01990 is Pune Metro's WhatsApp ticket number. Maha Metro lists it on its website for the Purple and Aqua lines, and Punekar News reported that it books Line 3, the Pink Line, too. Puneri Metro lists QR tickets on WhatsApp among its ticket options.

Pune Metro Guide doesn't sell tickets. This is the operators' own service: your ticket and payment are between you and them.

Step by step

  1. Save +91 94201 01990 in your phone's contacts, with the +91 code, or tap “Open WhatsApp chat” at the top of this page.
  2. Send “Hi”.
  3. Tap “Book Now” and choose your line: Line 3 for the Pink Line, or Maha Metro's lines for the Purple and Aqua lines.
  4. Pick your starting station and your destination from the lists.
  5. Choose “Single Journey”, or “Group Journey” and the number of passengers. The fare appears straight away.
  6. Check the summary, tap “Pay Online” and pay by UPI or card. Finish paying within five minutes.
  7. Your QR ticket arrives in the chat. Scan it at the entry gate and keep it handy: you scan it again at the exit.

These steps follow Punekar News's walkthrough of 4 November 2026. The chat's buttons can change; if they look different, follow its prompts.

Trips that need two tickets

The Pink Line is run by Puneri Metro, and the Purple and Aqua lines by Maha Metro. A trip from one to the other needs a ticket for each part, and you change at District Court.

  • On WhatsApp, book two tickets: one for the Line 3 part, to or from District Court, and one for the Maha Metro part.
  • The official Pune Metro app sells both tickets in one payment.
  • At District Court, scan your first ticket to leave one operator's gates, then your second ticket at the other operator's gates.

For example, Hinjawadi Phase-I to Pune Railway Station:

Puneri Metro: Hinjawadi Phase-I → District Court₹49
Maha Metro: District Court → Pune Railway Station₹10
Total₹59

Punekar News reported on 4 November 2026 that joining up the two ticket systems is expected to take another three to four months.

If something goes wrong

  • Paid, but no ticket? Contact the operator of the line you booked. Purple and Aqua lines: Maha Metro customer care, 1800 270 5501 or customercare.pmrp@mahametro.org. Pink Line: Puneri Metro, support@punerimetro.in.
  • Ran out of time to pay? Send “Hi” and book again.
  • Low battery? Your phone is your ticket, and you need the QR code at the exit gate too.
  • No data, or no WhatsApp? Buy at the station: every line has ticket counters and ticket machines.

Other ways to buy

  • The official Pune Metro app (Android, iPhone) sells tickets for every line, and a two-ticket trip in one payment.
  • Other apps, through ONDC: Paytm, PhonePe, Uber, Amazon and Flipkart, for the Purple and Aqua lines.
  • At the station: ticket counters and ticket machines.
  • One Pune cards cut the fare on every Maha Metro ride: 10% off with the One Pune NCMC RuPay card and 30% off with the One Pune Vidyarthi Pass. Puneri Metro accepts One Pune cards on the Pink Line.

Questions

What is the Pune Metro WhatsApp number?

+91 94201 01990. Send “Hi” to start booking.

Can I book a Pink Line (Line 3) ticket on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the same number and choose Line 3 in the chat.

Can I book for a group on WhatsApp?

Yes. Choose “Group Journey” and enter the number of passengers.

How do I pay for a WhatsApp ticket?

Online, by UPI or card, within five minutes of booking.

Do I need two tickets from Hinjawadi to Swargate?

Yes: one for Line 3 to District Court and one for the Purple Line from there. From Hinjawadi Phase-I that's ₹49 + ₹15 = ₹64. The Hinjawadi guide has every fare.

How to ride

From the station entrance to the platform, step by step. · 1 min read

  1. Find the station entrance. Station pages list the gates and which side of the road they open onto.
  2. Buy a QR ticket for your destination on WhatsApp, in the official app or another ONDC app, or at the counter. A Pink Line trip to or from the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets.
  3. Go through the security check, then scan the QR code at the entry gate.
  4. Follow the signs for the platform towards the end of the line you're heading to (for example, “towards Swargate”).
  5. At your stop, scan the same QR code at the exit gate. Keep it until you're out.

QR tickets

WhatsApp, the official app, other apps or the counter. · 1 min read

Cards and discounts

One Pune cards and what they save you. · 1 min read

Maha Metro offers discounts on the Purple and Aqua lines with One Pune cards:

  • One Pune NCMC RuPay card: 10% off every Maha Metro ride
  • One Pune Vidyarthi Pass: 30% off every Maha Metro ride

Ask at a Maha Metro ticket counter for how to get a card. We have not found a published Pink Line card discount.

Changing lines

District Court, Shivaji Nagar and the two-ticket rule. · 1 min read

  • Shivaji Nagar Purple Line Pink Line
    The Pink Line platform isn't open yet, so you can't change here for now.
  • District Court Purple Line Aqua Line Pink Line
    Change between Maha Metro and Puneri Metro here. You'll need a second ticket for the other operator's line.

A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both.

Accessibility

Lifts, escalators and accessible toilets. · 1 min read

  • Maha Metro lists lifts from the entrance to the platform, and accessible toilets, among its station facilities, but not station by station.
  • Puneri Metro lists lifts, escalators, and separate and accessible toilets at its stations, and parking at some of them.

Station pages list lifts and escalators where the operators publish them, station by station.

Questions

What are the Pune Metro timings?

First trains leave at 06:00. Maha Metro runs the Purple and Aqua lines until 23:00. On the Pink Line the last trains leave Maan at 22:30 and District Court at 23:40. All timings.

How much is the Pune Metro fare?

₹10 to ₹35 on the Purple and Aqua lines, and ₹12 to ₹62 on the Pink Line, from the operators' official charts. Fare calculator · fare charts.

Is the Pink Line (Line 3) open?

Yes, since 4 November 2026, from Maan to District Court, with 19 of 23 stations open. Pink Line stations.

Do I need two tickets?

Only between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line: they are run by different operators. A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both.

Can I buy a Pune Metro ticket on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send "Hi" to 94201 01990 on WhatsApp (+91). Step by step · all the ways to buy.

Is Pune Metro Guide the official website?

No. It's an independent guide built from the operators' published fares and timings, and it doesn't sell tickets. The official sites are punemetrorail.org (Maha Metro) and punerimetro.in (Puneri Metro).

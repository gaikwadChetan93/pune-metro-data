      Questions people ask

      What are the Pune Metro timings?

      First trains leave at 06:00. Maha Metro runs the Purple and Aqua lines until 23:00. On the Pink Line the last trains leave Maan at 22:30 and District Court at 23:40. All timings.

      How much is the Pune Metro fare?

      ₹10 to ₹35 on the Purple and Aqua lines, and ₹12 to ₹62 on the Pink Line, from the operators' official charts. Fare calculator · fare charts.

      Is the Pink Line (Line 3) open?

      Yes, since 4 November 2026, from Maan to District Court, with 17 of 23 stations open. Pink Line stations.

      Do I need two tickets?

      Only between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line: they are run by different operators. A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both.

      Can I buy a Pune Metro ticket on WhatsApp?

      Yes. Send "Hi" to 94201 01990 on WhatsApp (+91). Step by step · all the ways to buy.

      Is Pune Metro Guide the official website?

      No. It's an independent guide built from the operators' published fares and timings, and it doesn't sell tickets. The official sites are punemetrorail.org (Maha Metro) and punerimetro.in (Puneri Metro).

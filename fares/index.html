Pune Metro fare chart: every station, all lines

Fares come from the operators' official charts: Maha Metro for the Purple and Aqua lines (published 2026-02-09) and Puneri Metro for the Pink Line (published 2026-10-29). Fares are in rupees. Pick a station's page for its fares to every other station, including two-ticket trips.

A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both.

Purple and Aqua lines (Maha Metro)

The official Maha Metro chart prints a different fare in each direction for a few station pairs, so those show as a range.

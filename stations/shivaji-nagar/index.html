Shivaji Nagar metro station: timings, fares and exits

शिवाजीनगर

Shivaji Nagar is a Pune Metro station on the Purple and Pink lines, and an interchange. It is open; its Pink Line platform isn't open yet. Open in maps

Also known as: Shivajinagar

Lifts5
Escalators14 (12 installed)

Questions about Nashik Phata (Bhosari)

Which line is Nashik Phata (Bhosari) metro station on?

Nashik Phata (Bhosari) is on the Purple Line. It is open.

What are the first and last train times at Nashik Phata (Bhosari)?

The Purple Line runs from 06:00 to 23:00; Maha Metro doesn't publish the last train from each station.

How much is the metro fare from Nashik Phata (Bhosari)?

From ₹10 to ₹87 to the other open stations, from the operators' official charts. Trips onto another operator's line need a second ticket.

Is there parking at Nashik Phata (Bhosari) metro station?

No parking. Maha Metro hasn't put parking at its Pimpri-Chinchwad stations; paid parking announced in 2024 didn't start.

What is near Nashik Phata (Bhosari) metro station?

Kasarwadi railway station (157 m), Pimpri-Chinchwad (Vallabhnagar) bus stand (780 m), measured in a straight line.

Questions about Bund Garden

Which line is Bund Garden metro station on?

Bund Garden is on the Aqua Line. It is open.

What are the first and last train times at Bund Garden?

The Aqua Line runs from 06:00 to 23:00; Maha Metro doesn't publish the last train from each station.

How much is the metro fare from Bund Garden?

From ₹10 to ₹77 to the other open stations, from the operators' official charts. Trips onto another operator's line need a second ticket.

What is near Bund Garden metro station?

Bund Garden (143 m), Jewel Square Mall (322 m), Osho International Meditation Resort (672 m), Inlaks and Budhrani Hospital (745 m), measured in a straight line.

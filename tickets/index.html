How to buy Pune Metro tickets: WhatsApp, app and counter

Pune Metro Guide doesn't sell tickets. These are the official ways to buy them, as the operators and news reports describe them.

A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both.

Maha Metro

Purple Line · Aqua Line

  • WhatsApp: send “Hi” to +91 94201 01990
  • Official app: Android · iPhone
  • Other apps (ONDC): Paytm, PhonePe, Uber, Amazon, Flipkart
  • At the station: ticket counter or machine
  • Discounts: One Pune NCMC RuPay card 10%; One Pune Vidyarthi Pass 30%

Puneri Metro

Pink Line

  • WhatsApp: send “Hi” to +91 94201 01990
  • Official app: Android · iPhone
  • At the station: ticket counter or machine

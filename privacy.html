Privacy policy

Pune Metro Guide for Android and iOS · Effective 8 November 2026

The short version

  • The app doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.
  • There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your saved journeys, saved stations, recent stations and settings stay on your device.

Who makes the app

Pune Metro Guide is an independent, unofficial guide to the Pune Metro, made by TrueZen. It isn't affiliated with Maha Metro, Puneri Metro or PMRDA, and it doesn't sell tickets.

What stays on your device

The app keeps these in its own private storage on your phone:

  • journeys and stations you save,
  • stations you searched for recently,
  • your settings, such as theme and reduce motion, and
  • the latest metro data (stations, timings and fares).

None of this is sent anywhere. You can clear recent stations in Settings, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything.

What the app downloads

The app works offline. When you're online, about twice a day, and when you tap Check for updates in Settings, it downloads public metro data files from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).

These requests contain nothing about you: no name, no account, no device identifier and none of your saved journeys. As with any website you visit, GitHub receives your IP address and basic technical details of the request. The app doesn't receive or keep them. GitHub handles them under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links you tap

Links to the official ticketing apps, WhatsApp ticketing, data sources and helpline numbers open in other apps only when you tap them. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

On Android, the app asks only for internet access, to download metro data. It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, open an issue at github.com/gaikwadChetan93/pune-metro-data/issues.

Pune Metro timings

Timings come from the operators' official timetables and notices and are the same every day of the week. On festivals and election days the operators sometimes change the hours.

Service notices

Neither operator publishes live running information. For disruptions and special hours, check punemetrorail.org, punerimetro.in or the notices at the station.

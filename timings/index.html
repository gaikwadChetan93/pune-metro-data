Pune Metro timings: first and last trains

Timings come from the operators' official timetables and notices. They are the same every day of the week; on festivals and election days the operators sometimes change the hours, which the app shows as special hours.

Purple Line

First train06:00
Service hours06:00–23:00
Last trainMaha Metro doesn't publish the last train from each station; service ends at 23:00.
How oftenPeak (08:00-11:00, 16:00-20:00): every 6 min; Other times: every 10 min (unverified)

Pink Line

First train06:00
Last train22:30 from Maan; 23:40 from District Court
How oftenPeak (07:30-09:30, 17:00-20:00): every 5.5 min; Other times: every 10 min
End to end45 min, 23.3 km (Maan – District Court)

The app estimates the last Pink Line train at each station from these official departures, and can remind you before it leaves.

Puneri Metro

Two operators, one network

Maha Metro runs the Purple and Aqua lines. Puneri Metro, a PMRDA concession operated by Keolis, runs the Pink Line. Each sells its own tickets.

A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both.

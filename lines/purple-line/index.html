Purple Line: stations, timings and fares

The Purple Line runs from PCMC to Swargate and is operated by Maha Metro (Maharashtra Metro Rail Corporation Ltd). 13 of its 14 stations are open. Fares run from ₹10 to ₹35 (official chart).

First train06:00
Service hours06:00–23:00
Last trainMaha Metro doesn't publish the last train from each station; service ends at 23:00.
How oftenPeak (08:00-11:00, 16:00-20:00): every 6 min; Other times: every 10 min (unverified)

A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both. Where to buy tickets.

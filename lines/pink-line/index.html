Pink Line (Line 3): stations, timings and fares

The Pink Line runs from Maan to District Court and is operated by Puneri Metro (Pune IT City Metro Rail Ltd, PMRDA concession; operated by Keolis). 17 of its 23 stations are open. Fares run from ₹12 to ₹62 (official chart).

First train06:00
Last train22:30 from Maan; 23:40 from District Court
How oftenPeak (07:30-09:30, 17:00-20:00): every 5.5 min; Other times: every 10 min
End to end45 min, 23.3 km (Maan – District Court)

Latest

The Pink Line is open. Maan to District Court, with 17 of 23 stations open. First trains leave both ends at 06:00. Metro Rail News

Six Pink Line stations are still closed. Balewadi High Street and Balewadi Phata are expected by end of November. Baner Gaon, Baner Pashan Link Road, Bhosalenagar - RBI and Shivaji Nagar are expected in phases by January 2027. Metro Rail News (expected dates)

The Pink Line needs its own ticket. Trips between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line use two QR tickets. The official app sells both in one payment. Pune Metro official app release notes

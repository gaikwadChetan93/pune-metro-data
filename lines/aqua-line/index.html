Aqua Line: Vanaz – Ramwadi

The Aqua Line runs from Vanaz to Ramwadi and is operated by Maha Metro (Maharashtra Metro Rail Corporation Ltd). 16 of its 16 stations are open. Fares run from ₹10 to ₹35 (official chart).

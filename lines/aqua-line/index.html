Aqua Line: stations, timings and fares

The Aqua Line runs from Vanaz to Ramwadi and is operated by Maha Metro (Maharashtra Metro Rail Corporation Ltd). 16 of its 16 stations are open. Fares run from ₹10 to ₹35 (official chart).

First train06:00
Service hours06:00–23:00
Last trainMaha Metro doesn't publish the last train from each station; service ends at 23:00.
How oftenPeak (08:00-11:00, 16:00-20:00): every 6 min; Other times: every 10 min (unverified)

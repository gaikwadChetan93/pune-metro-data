Hinjawadi Phase-I → Pune Railway Station

  1. Board the Pink Line at Hinjawadi Phase-I towards District Court. Ride 9 stops to District Court.
  2. Change to the Aqua Line at District Court towards Ramwadi. Ride 2 stops to Pune Railway Station.
  • Puneri Metro: Hinjawadi Phase-I → District Court, ₹49
  • Maha Metro: District Court → Pune Railway Station, ₹10

Pune Railway Station → Hinjawadi Phase-I

  1. Board the Aqua Line at Pune Railway Station towards Vanaz. Ride 2 stops to District Court.
  2. Change to the Pink Line at District Court towards Maan. Ride 9 stops to Hinjawadi Phase-I.
  • Maha Metro: Pune Railway Station → District Court, ₹10
  • Puneri Metro: District Court → Hinjawadi Phase-I, ₹49

A trip between the Pink Line and the Purple or Aqua Line needs two tickets: one from Puneri Metro and one from Maha Metro. The fare is the sum of both.
